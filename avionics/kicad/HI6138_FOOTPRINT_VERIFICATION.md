| 47 | BENDI | no-connect | 50 kΩ pull-down, so the SPI is little-endian |

| 49 | EP | GND | "connect to any ground or power plane" |

**Oscillator `X-50M`:** ECS-2520MV-500-BN-TR (ECS part-numbering guide: 500 = 50.000 MHz,
B = ± 50 ppm, N = −40 to +85 °C). Pads are 1 TRI (no-connect, meaning active), 2 GND, 3 OUT,
and 4 VDD (+3V3), with a 100 nF bypass `C-50M`. The ±50 ppm stability is within the ±100 ppm
MCLK requirement. This is the same family and footprint as the fleet's `X-25M`.

**Decoupling.** DS6138 gives no capacitor values. One 100 nF capacitor per supply group plus
the existing 10 µF bulk capacitor is an engineering choice (plan KTD5), not a datasheet
citation.

## 4. Courtyard area budget (per cape)

Courtyards are read from the loaded KiCad footprints using `GetCourtyard(F_CrtYd)`, following
convention rule 3.

| Change | Part | Courtyard (mm²) |
|---|---|---|
| Removed | HI-1573, QFN-44 7 × 7 mm | 69.72 |
| Added | HI-6138, QFN-48 6 × 6 mm | 53.14 |
| Added | ECS-2520MV 50 MHz oscillator | 9.55 |
| Added | C-50M, 0402 | 1.93 |
| Added | R-1553IRQ, 0402 | 2.01 |
| Added | C-1553D, 0402 | 1.93 |
| **Net** | | **−1.16** |

The swap is area-neutral to slightly negative on both capes. The HI-6138 fits inside the old
QFN-44 courtyard with 0.8 mm to spare on every side. The four small parts are placed by a
courtyard-aware search in Phases 2 and 3, and nothing else on the board moves.
