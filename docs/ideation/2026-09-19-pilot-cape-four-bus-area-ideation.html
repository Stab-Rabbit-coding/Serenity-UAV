Grounding Context

Codebase context. Pilot is a PocketBeagle 2 Industrial cape, 55 × 35 mm (2.165 × 1.378 in), 4-layer, stacked on two 2×18 0.1 in THT sockets on B.Cu at v = 2.54 / 32.46 mm; the usable band between the rails is v 5.7–29.3 mm ≈ 1300 mm² per side. The node envelope is fixed by tools/cargo_layout_fit.py (NODE_L, NODE_H, NODE_T = 58.0, 37.0, 22.0 pouch-inclusive; NODE_CABLE = 10.0 connector-edge clearance) and the four nodes already had to be split between the chin shelf and the aft strip (docs/CARGO_SECTION_LAYOUT.md). Architecture: docs/AVIONICS_PB2_REDESIGN.md §3 "All 8 nodes participate equally on all 4 wired data buses" and §3.1 line 118 "U8 | DP83825I × 2 | CPSW3G RMII | 10/100 Ethernet PHY". Rev T content as generated by avionics/kicad/Pilot/scripts/gen_pilot_sch.py (ERC 0): ISOW1044 + ISOW1412 (SOIC-20W, 160 mm² courtyard each), 2 × ADIN1300 (54 each) + 2 × Würth 749010012A magnetics (146 each), HI-1573 QFN-44 (54) + PM-DB2791S 1:2.5 transformer (150) + 2 × SMAJ33CA + 2 × 55 Ω, MAX-M10S GPS + U.FL + bias-T (155), SLB9672 TPM (40), IMU, baro, TPS62933 buck, TLV75801 0.9 V LDO, 2 × 50 MHz oscillators, Nano-Fit power (THT, 87), 5 × JST GH4 horizontal (62 each = 310), one 4×4 0.1 in THT PWM/DSHOT header with PGND shield row (≈130 mm² on both sides), ≈110 passives (≈450). Measured: ≈2480 of ≈2600 mm² → 95 % both sides; 1 mm free-cell map 100 % black; 4 caps unplaceable; only F/B carry signals (inner layers are planes).

Constraints (directive). Four-bus presence on every Pilot and XO node; PWM/DSHOT header with PGND shield row retained; no second cape; OEM datasheet for every part; MIL-STD-1553B-compliant 1553 port; DRC-clean fabricable gerbers.

Verification. All candidates were checked by a fresh-context verifier; three were refuted outright and are listed in the rejection summary. Independent corroboration counts only where a separate dispatched context produced it — the orchestrator's own cross-cutting combinations were verified by that agent, not self-attested.

Topic Axes

  1. Connector & interconnect footprint (5 GH4 + Nano-Fit + 4×4 THT header ≈ 570 mm²).
  2. Bus-transceiver & magnetics packaging, incl. smaller IC packages (≈ 770 mm²).
  3. Form factor / stack / mechanics inside the 58 × 37 × 22 mm node envelope.
  4. Function partitioning across the fleet while keeping four-bus presence on every node.
  5. PCB technology & passives (layer count, via technology, 0201, arrays).

Ranked Ideas

  1. Go SMT-only on the stack hardware and 6-layer
  2. Return the PHY to the DP83825I the redesign specified
  3. One isolated island, not two
  4. Treat the 1553 stage as one unit — and decide single vs dual
  5. Passive diet: 0201 straps and resistor arrays
  6. Consolidate field connectors in tiers
  7. Make Ethernet a real multidrop bus (10BASE-T1S)

1. Go SMT-only on the stack hardware and 6-layer — zero electrical change

description
Replace the two THT 2×18 PB2 stacking sockets with surface-mount 0.100 in receptacles (Samtec SSM series), the 4×4 THT PWM/DSHOT header with a surface-mount 0.100 in terminal strip (Samtec TSM series — still 0.1 in, still SIG / +5V / GND / PGND-shield columns, so standard ESC leads plug in), and the Nano-Fit with its SMT variant. Then respin the stackup to 6 layers (F · GND · sig · sig · +3V3 · B) and drop the via to 0.15 mm drill. Gains: the F.Cu bands over the two rails (≈ 2 × 47 × 5.7 ≈ 530 mm²) become placeable for 0402 bypass parts that already want to sit next to the header nets; the B.Cu under the PWM header (≈ 130 mm²) frees; two more signal layers turn "95 % full and unroutable" into routable. Cost: SMT sockets have lower retention — use the four M2.5 PGND standoffs already in the design as the mechanical retention; solder-mask-defined pads; ≈1.5× bare-board price for 6-layer; gen_pilot_pcb.py:420 hardcodes SetCopperLayerCount(4) and must change.
axis
3 / 5
basis
direct: "stacks on PB2 via two 2x18 0.1 in sockets on B.Cu … usable band v 5.7–29.3 mm" and "one 4x4 0.1 in THT PWM/DSHOT header … ≈ 130 mm² on BOTH sides (THT)" (grounding); external: Samtec SSM (SMT 0.100 in socket strips) and TSM (SMT 0.100 in terminal strips) — verified real by the verifier; PC/104 and VITA 42 XMC budget the stacking connector to one face; JLCPCB 6-layer, 0.15 mm drill.
rationale
It is the only idea that adds ≈ 660 mm² of usable area and doubles signal-routing capacity without touching a single net, bus, part value or the harness — so it is also the only one that carries no architecture decision.
downsides
Socket retention (mitigated by standoffs); 6-layer cost and lead; the SMT socket/header MPNs and JLCPCB assembly support must be confirmed from datasheets before the generator changes.

2. Return the PHY to the DP83825I the redesign specified — and delete the 0.9 V rail

description
docs/AVIONICS_PB2_REDESIGN.md §3.1 line 118 specifies "U8 | DP83825I × 2 | CPSW3G RMII | 10/100 Ethernet PHY, ring port A and port B". The board drifted to the gigabit ADIN1300 (LFCSP-40 6 × 6 mm, 54 mm² courtyard each), which drags in the TLV75801 0.9 V LDO + divider + 8 core caps, 12 hardware-strap resistors and two 50 MHz oscillators. DP83825I is a 10/100 RMII PHY in WQFN-24 3 × 3 mm (≈ 16 mm² courtyard, verifier corrected the "9 mm²" body-vs-courtyard comparison), single 3.3 V supply, and in RMII-master mode generates the 50 MHz REF_CLK itself from a 25 MHz crystal — so the two 50 MHz oscillators become two 25 MHz crystals or one shared 25 MHz oscillator, and the REF_CLK direction question at the SoC goes away. Keeps the 749010012A magnetics and the ring topology. Saves ≈ 2 × 38 mm² + LDO/passives ≈ 110–130 mm² and ≈ 15 placed parts on a board whose passives are the largest single block.
axis
2
basis
direct: "| U8 | DP83825I × 2 | CPSW3G RMII | 10/100 Ethernet PHY, ring port A and port B |" (docs/AVIONICS_PB2_REDESIGN.md:118, verified verbatim); external: TI DP83825I datasheet — WQFN-24 3 × 3 mm, RMII master mode sources REF_CLK from a 25 MHz input (verifier-confirmed).
rationale
The 100BASE-TX ring never needed a gigabit PHY; this is drift, not design — and the deletion of an entire supply rail is the rare change that removes routing as well as area.
downsides
Gives up ADIN1300's industrial EMI-robustness positioning (ADI claim); the Pilot.md EMI narrative and the ADIN1300 footprint work of this session are superseded; PHY straps/REF_CLK mode must be transcribed from the DP83825I datasheet before the generator changes.

3. One isolated island, not two — and say what the barrier really is

description
Keep the ISOW1044 (its VISOOUT has +20 mA of spare isolated current per isow1044.pdf, verifier-confirmed) and drop the ISOW1412 in favour of a THVD1424 RS-485 transceiver (VSON-10 3 × 3) plus an ISO7731 3-channel digital isolator (QSOP-16) powered from that same isolated rail. GND2_CAN and GND2_RS485 merge into one island; six island-side bulk caps become three; the second integrated DC-DC (a heat source inside a foil pouch) is gone. Saves ≈ 110 mm² and one isolation moat. Like-for-like fallback if two islands are wanted: ADM2687E (16-lead SOIC-W 10 × 13 mm, 5 kV, isoPower) saves ≈ 30 mm² with no other change. Owner decision attached: the cape can only hold a 0.5 mm ISOLATION moat; IEC 60664-1 basic insulation alone needs ≈ 0.56 mm at 250 V working and reinforced far more, so "5 kV reinforced" is a component rating the board cannot deliver. Either the fleet isolation claim in the redesign doc is restated as functional isolation at the cape (with real creepage at the harness couplers / power entry, industrial-fieldbus practice), or the aggressive variant applies: non-isolated fault-tolerant TCAN1044V-Q1 (SOT-23-8) + THVD1424 with TVS/CMC, ≈ −270 mm², the automotive-gateway model. The 1553 and Ethernet ports remain transformer-isolated either way.
axis
2 / 5
basis
direct: "board can only offer a 0.5 mm ISOLATION netclass moat (Pilot.md target 8 mm creepage unattainable on this cape)" (grounding); direct: ISOW1044 "additional output current" +20 mA (avionics/datasheets/isow1044.pdf, verifier); reasoned: isolation rating is set by the weakest element in the barrier, so a 5 kV device on a 0.5 mm gap yields the gap's rating.
rationale
Directly answers the smaller-package question with the two largest ICs on the board, and removing plane islands is what makes the inner layers useful again.
downsides
CAN and RS-485 lose isolation from each other (remain isolated from the SoC); THVD1424 + ISO7731 side-2 current must be checked against the 20 mA budget; the aggressive variant is a fleet-level safety/EMC decision and must be written into AVIONICS_PB2_REDESIGN.md, not slipped in.

4. Treat the 1553 stage as one unit — and decide single vs dual

description
Rev T carries a dual-bus HI-1573 with a single PM-DB2791S transformer — the redesign doc's 1553 stage (DS26LV31/32 + one T1) was single-bus, so the BOM is currently neither single nor dual-redundant (verifier-confirmed mismatch). Decide: (a) single bus → a single-channel Holt 3.3 V transceiver if one exists in a smaller package (none verified this pass; HI-1567 alternates are not smaller than 7 × 7), otherwise keep HI-1573 with bus B strapped idle; or (b) dual-redundant → add the second transformer (+150 mm²) and connector, which only the SMT/6-layer and PHY ideas above make affordable. Independently of that choice: place HI-1573 + transformer + 55 Ω + TVS as one fixed, guard-ringed island in gen_pilot_pcb.py rather than letting the autoplacer scatter it (stub length and waveform matter to MIL-STD-1553B §4.5.2). Optional export: aircraft practice puts 1553 magnetics in potted in-line couplers, and the direct-coupled stub definition (§4.5.1.5.2) constrains stub length and waveform at the bus, not where inside the terminal the transformer sits — a potted pigtail coupler carrying transformer + 55 Ω + TVS would remove ≈ 200 mm² and the tallest part from the pouch, at the cost of bench-verifying the rise-time window with ~100 mm of unshielded drive.
axis
2 / 4
basis
direct: "MIL-STD-1553B: Holt HI-1573 (QFN-44 7x7, 3.3 V, dual-bus transceiver) + Premier Magnetics PM-DB2791S 1:2.5 xfmr" (grounding) vs docs/AVIONICS_PB2_REDESIGN.md "U7 | DS26LV31 + DS26LV32 … T1 | PE-68515 (or equiv)" (single stage); reasoned: MIL-STD-1553B §4.5.1.5.2 defines the direct-coupled stub by transformer ratio, 55 Ω isolation resistors and bus-side waveform, not by packaging location.
rationale
The owner's non-negotiable bus deserves a deliberate topology decision and a compliant, compact layout instead of an accidental half-dual footprint.
downsides
Dual redundancy costs area the board does not have unless ideas 1–2 land first; the potted coupler adds an assembly and a test.

5. Passive diet: 0201 straps and pull-ups, resistor arrays, via-in-pad decoupling

description
≈ 110 passives (≈ 450 mm²) out-weigh any IC block. Move the ≈ 70 pull-ups, straps and series terminations from 0402 to 0201 (≈ 1.6 mm² courtyard each saved ≈ 110 mm²), collapse the MDIO/RMII/SPI pull-ups into 4-element 0402 arrays (4 : 1), and — with the 6-layer/0.15 mm-via stack of idea 1 — put decoupling via-in-pad directly under the QFNs. Net ≈ 180–220 mm². Both Pilot and XO generators inherit it through the shared footprint constants in gen_pilot_sch.py.
axis
5
basis
direct: "≈110 passives (0402/0603/0805/1210/1808) ≈ 450 mm²" (grounding); reasoned: the pin count driving the passives is fixed by the four-bus + sensor set, so package size and array integration are the only free variables.
rationale
The one lever that needs no architecture decision and compounds with every other idea.
downsides
0201 hand-rework is impractical (JLCPCB assembly only); via-in-pad needs filled/capped vias (cost tier); array pull-ups tie value choices together.

6. Consolidate field connectors in tiers

description
Tier 1: fold the five GH4 shells into two shared shells (CAN + RS-485 in one GH8; ETH1 + ETH2 in another), same family and crimp tooling — a GH8 horizontal is ≈ 14.5 × 6.4 mm vs 2 × 9.5 × 6.4, saving ≈ 60 mm² plus two shell-to-shell keepouts. Tier 2: one multi-way 1.25 mm shrouded header (Hirose DF13 2×13 class) carrying power (paralleled pins), all four buses and shield drains, with a ground pin between every pair — the automotive-ECU single-connector pattern, ≈ −290 mm² and one pouch penetration instead of six; requires a harness breakout. Smaller locking families if GH is kept per bus: Molex Pico-Clasp (1.0 mm, positive lock) or JST SUR (0.8 mm, IDC with lock); JST SH has no lock and is out. Measured, not assumed: vertical GH4 (BM04B) is 62.4 mm² vs 60.5 mm² horizontal — orientation buys nothing.
axis
1
basis
direct: "5x JST GH4 horizontal field connectors CAN, RS-485, 1553, ETH1, ETH2 (9.55 x 6.5 = 62 each = 310)" (grounding); external: Hirose DF13 double-row 1.25 mm headers; TE MCON/MQS multi-way ECU headers as the pattern; Molex Pico-Clasp / JST SUR datasheets (web researcher).
rationale
Connectors are the one category spending ≈ 570 mm² on mechanics rather than electronics, and the harness is a ladder (every node taps every bus), not a star.
downsides
Tier 2 mixes 1553 drive levels with Ethernet pairs in one shell and puts power on signal-class pins (derating); the 1553 stub shield must still be bonded to PGND within a few mm of the transformer; the fleet harness drawings change.

7. Make Ethernet a real multidrop bus: 10BASE-T1S as the failover presence

description
The architecture is four parallel multidrop buses; 1553, CAN and RS-485 are, but Ethernet is the odd one — two switched point-to-point ports per node. IEEE 802.3cg 10BASE-T1S supports ≥ 8 nodes on one 25 m pair with PLCA and no switch: one Microchip LAN8670 (32-VQFN 5 × 5, RMII on the existing RMII0 pins), one CMC, one connector replaces both PHYs, both magnetics, both oscillators and the second GH4 — ≈ −480 mm². Cost: 10 Mb/s, so this is the failover Ethernet presence; the Observer's camera streams keep their own 100 Mb link, and AVIONICS_PB2_REDESIGN.md must say which Ethernet flavour is the fleet backbone (the KSZ9477 HSR/PRP ring is the current answer).
axis
4
basis
external: IEEE 802.3cg-2019 Clause 147/148 (10BASE-T1S multidrop, PLCA); Microchip LAN8670 datasheet, 32-VQFN (verifier-confirmed real); direct: "All 8 nodes participate equally on all 4 wired data buses" (docs/AVIONICS_PB2_REDESIGN.md).
rationale
It matches the stated architecture better than the current habit, and it is the largest single area win found — but it is an architecture change, so it ranks last until the owner wants that conversation.
downsides
Fleet-wide PHY/harness change; 10 Mb/s only; conflicts with the HSR/PRP ring unless that ring is redefined as the high-rate payload network.
