# Exhaustive, systematic in-context joint analysis of the airframe

## 1. Owner direction (2026-10-03)

> "the joint analysis needs to be exhaustive and systematic."
>
> "the geometry assembly needs to inform, but not rule the airframe assembly
> for each joint. … don't make a correction to one wing that makes it at a
> different longitudinal position than the other one."

The joints named earlier (nose pitot line, nacelle ESC cooling, tilt joint)
were examples, not the scope. The scope is **every** interface on the
aircraft.

## 2. Governing rules

- **R1. Exhaustive by construction, not by recall.** The list of interfaces
  is generated by testing every pair of placed parts. Nobody hand-picks it.
  A part that has no placed geometry is a coverage gap, not an exemption.
- **R2. The airframe assembly is the authority on position.**
  `airframe/FreeCAD-scripts/serenity_assembly.py` decides where each part
  sits. Local context files, such as `nacelle_tilt_joint_context.scad`,
  check fit and motion using measured local geometry, and their results are
  compared against the airframe placement.
- **R3. Local fixes flow through shared parameters only.** A correction
  goes into the single-source parameter that both sides and the airframe
  read: for example `wings_s1223_revo.scad` (mirrored), the station tables
  in `tools/cargo_layout_fit.py`, or the nacelle wrapper constants. A fix is
  never a one-off placement of a single part, a single side, or a single
  context file.
- **R4. Symmetry is gated.** Every port/starboard twin is mirrored and
  compared. A desync greater than 0.5 mm fails the gate unless the asymmetry
  is deliberate (for example `SWIRL_DIR`) and recorded in the register.
- **R5. Non-printed parts and air paths are first-class solids.** This
  covers PCBs at their real stack height, connectors with mating space,
  bearings, magnets, sensors, inserts and fasteners with tool access, and
  harnesses as swept tubes with bend radius and service loops across moving
  joints. It also covers keep-out solids for air paths: pitot and static
  lines, ESC cooling inlets and discharge ports, intakes, exhaust, the
  battery vent, and antenna keep-outs.
- **R6. Motion is swept, not sampled at rest.** Tilt, nozzle petals, doors,
  the winch, the release mechanism and landing-gear compression are each
  checked over their full range.

## 3. Method

| Step | Tool | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Export placed parts | `freecadcmd tools/export_assembly_meshes.py -- DIR` | hull-frame STL per placed part plus a manifest |
| 2. Pair census | `tools/airframe_interface_census.py DIR` | every pair classified as CLASH, CONTACT or NEAR (≤ 3 mm); a symmetry table |
| 3. Coverage | census extension: BOM ↔ placed-part map | BOM lines, harnesses and air paths with no placed envelope = GAP |
| 4. Register | `docs/AIRFRAME_JOINT_REGISTER.md` | one row per interface: type, motion, mating datums, non-printed parts, air path, owning check, status |
| 5. Local in-context checks | per joint (pattern: `nacelle_tilt_joint_context.scad` with a manifold3d sweep) | fit and motion verdict, measured on local geometry |
| 6. Reconcile | compare the step 5 placement with the step 1 placement | any difference is fixed at the shared parameter (R3), then steps 1–2 are re-run |
| 7. Gate | CI or pre-commit runs steps 1–3 | no CLASH, no DESYNC, no unregistered interface, no GAP |

## 4. Phases

- **P1 — Census baseline (this session).** Export the airframe, run the
  census and the symmetry check, and record the results in the register.
- **P2 — Coverage.** Map every BOM line to a placed solid. Author envelope
  solids for every non-printed part and harness segment, and keep-out solids
  for every air path. Wire them into the assembly through shared parameters.
- **P3 — Motion.** Add the swept-volume variants (tilt −5 to 140°, nozzle
  open and closed, doors, winch and release, gear compression) to the
  export, and re-run the census at each motion state.
- **P4 — Per-joint verdicts.** For every register row, run its in-context
  check and close or open a WBS item.
- **P5 — Gate.** Add the census to CI with a pinned baseline.

## 5. Known constraint

The master assembly still places the 50 mm nacelles (`nacelle_*_revs.stl`).
The 64 mm pod is not yet baked into the hull frame (WBS NAC-64-GEOM-04).
P1 therefore describes the airframe as it is currently baked. The 64 mm
census follows the bake, and the bake must move both nacelles together
(R3/R4).
