Spar carry-through joint (Rev T1) -- the spar IS the root load path
  fixed CF spar 20 x 16.3, Z 438.9 mm^3
  spar bending at the ultimate root moment (14.79 N.m):
    sigma 33.69 MPa   FOS 8.9 vs the 300 MPa stand-in  (UNVERIFIED, DEP-1)
    this is the CANTILEVER bound -- the whole root moment taken by
    the spar alone.  The two-support bound (nacelle load reacted
    between the wingtip clamp and the wall) gives only 4.41 N.m,
    sigma 10.04 MPa.  The cantilever bound is quoted because it
    does not depend on the wing skin sharing load, which is not
    characterised for a bonded printed skin.

  fuselage-side socket, D20 bonded (projected bearing vs 5 MPa):
    THE BAY BOUNDS THIS, NOT THE DESIGNER.  Owner requirement:
    the centre of the cargo bay stays clear.  The bay's clear span
    begins at hull X -100.0 and the wall skin sits at
    X -81.33, so the socket has 18.67 mm of depth and no more.
      L mm      F N  area mm2  sigma MPa    FOS
     18.67   1245.6     124.5     10.008   0.50  <- all the bay allows
     30.00    796.9     200.0      3.985   1.25  (would enter the bay)
     55.00    460.8     366.7      1.257   3.98  (would enter the bay)
    => the socket CANNOT carry the moment.  Stress goes as 1/L^2 and
       the bay has taken the length away.  Shear, however, is fine:
       sigma_shear = 115.1 / (20 x 18.5) = 0.311 MPa  -> FOS 16

  bonded ROOT FLANGE on the inner sidewall (moment path):
    Reacting the moment over wall AREA needs no inboard reach at all
    -- the flange lies flat against the wall and protrudes only its
    own thickness (~5 mm, to X ~ -86, against a bay edge at -100).
    Triangular pressure over height h, couple arm 2h/3:
      h mm   w mm      F N  area mm2  sigma MPa    FOS
        40     40    554.5       533      1.040    4.8
        60     50    369.7      1000      0.370   13.5
        80     60    277.3      1600      0.173   28.9  <- SPECIFIED
       100     60    221.8      2000      0.111   45.1
    80 x 60 is specified: FOS 29.2, and the cargo section is ~150 mm
    tall inside at this station so 80 mm of height is available.
    This is BETTER than the 55 mm socket it replaces (FOS 4.0), not a
    compromise -- a flange trades an unfavourable 1/L^2 depth term
    for a linear area term.

    LG-11 coupon sensitivity (flange 80 x 60):
        5.0 MPa -> FOS  28.9   (standing figure, structural_analysis.md SS7.3)
       15.0 MPa -> FOS  86.6   (the owner's <15 MPa fusion-strength decision rule)
       47.0 MPa -> FOS 271.2   (REF-MAT-001 ASTM D695 bulk compressive, 20 % CF-PETG)
      The coupon no longer gates BUILDABILITY here -- it only sets
      how small the flange could shrink.

  wing torsion about the spar axis (the retired aft rod's only
