# Handoff: nacelle exterior canon rule (2026-11-02)

## Objective

On 2026-11-01 the owner applied this rule (now in `airframe/AGENTS.md`, "Geometry
Integrity") to the nacelle exterior: *"deviations from the canonical exterior
hull shall be minimized, and every deviation shall maintain the style and
character of the Serenity spaceship."* The nacelle must still be aerodynamically
sound, fully functional and weight-managed, in PA11, PA12 or PA CF. The owner
asked that the nozzle be included.

## Done (Claude Opus 5.5)

- **Intake cowl.** The cowl is now lofted per 5° azimuth: the lipE nose is kept,
  then a monotone Hermite meets the measured skin with G1 continuity at the
  earliest feasible station (Z 28–45 mm).
  - Code: `circular_intake_fairing()` and `cowl_herm()` in
    `airframe/openscad/nacelles/nacelle_pod_64mm_tandem.scad`.
  - Table: generated by `tools/nacelle_cowl_fair.py` into
    `airframe/openscad/nacelles/nacelle_cowl_fair_table.scad`. The pod SCAD
    asserts that the table's generator constants match its own `P64_*` values.
- **Canon evidence.**
  - Henning renders: `nick-henning-uvdisplay-engine.jpg`,
    `nick-henning-final-front-combine.jpg` and
    `nick-henning-final-backside-combine.jpg` [REF-CAD-002].
  - QMx Sheets 3/4 [REF-CAD-003].
  - Both are recorded in `REFERENCES.md`.
- **Deviation against the skin (Z 0–45, first-cut table).** Old ring: max excess
  19.6 mm, integral 243 mm². New cowl: 18.2 mm and 205 mm², with zero excess from
  Z 37 aft. The final table was not re-measured. Its loft is smaller (68.4 vs
  92.8 cm³ solid), which suggests lower excess, but that is unproven.
- **CFD.** Hover screen in `tools/nacelle_intake_cfd.py`, variants `lipE_cowl_*`.
  - First-cut cowl: equal to lipE, with 0% reversed flow and 4.2% loss at
    r/R 0.95. The old r 45.05 ring scored 2.1%; that gain was traded for canon.
  - Final-table bounding azimuths (az 310 and az 115): 0% reversed flow on
    the lip and bell, 4.2% and 4.0% loss at r/R 0.95, both converged.
- **Mass.** Measured with `tools/nacelle_mass_cg_64.py` on the first-cut render:
  pod shell 389.4 → 388.7 g in PA CF, CG +0.09 mm, 3.0 in gear clearance
  unchanged at +0.45 in.
- **Material.** DuraForm PA CF, per `docs/SLS_MATERIAL_SELECTION_3DSYSTEMS.md` §3
  item 4. Heat near the motors and ESCs rules out PA11 and PA12.
- **Nozzle.** The 64 mm iris (`nacelle_nozzle_iris_64mm.scad`) already matches
  the canon shingled exhaust can. No change was made.
- **Reviews.**
  - ce-code-review found and fixed: a G1 kink from the clamp, the license (now
    CERN-OHL-W, because the tool writes CAD), and a stale header.
  - ce-simplify-code found and fixed: a CFD mesh change that hit old variants,
    constant drift, and shell constants now reused from
    `tools/nacelle_hollow_profile.py`.
- **Learning.**
  `docs/solutions/design-patterns/fair-a-forced-exterior-deviation-into-the-measured-canonical-skin.md`.

## Open (WBS `airframe/wings-nacelles/WBS.md` NAC-64-CANON-01)

- **.b — re-bake the published pod STLs.** The published
  `nacelle_{stbd,port}_64mm.stl` are still the pre-change geometry. Re-baking is
  held on NAC-64-PIVOT-RC: the mass tool reports a PIVOT_Z residual of +9.1 mm,
  and HEAD already showed the same before this change. Bake once, after the pivot
  converges. The pipeline is `tools/prep_nacelle_64_bake.py` →
  `tools/bake_hull_frame.py`.
- **.c — canon notched petal trailing edge on the nozzle.** Not modelled.
  Evaluate it only with the 75–105% area schedule held.
- **.d — SLS powder escape.** The lip-ring cavity has 4 × 3 mm vents. Confirm
  with 3D Systems.
- **Inherited, not introduced here.** The full pod render has 63 non-manifold
  edges at Z 85–119 (the ESC bays); the committed STL is also not watertight.

## Traps

- `pgrep -f <pattern>` inside a wait loop matches the loop itself, so it never
  exits. Use `pgrep -x openscad`.
- Imported non-manifold STLs break OpenSCAD CGAL crops. Measure with trimesh
  instead: `trimesh.section(plane_origin=..., plane_normal=...)`, passed as
  keywords. The positional order is normal first.
- A full pod render takes about 25 min. The OpenFOAM screen takes about 5 min
  per variant.
