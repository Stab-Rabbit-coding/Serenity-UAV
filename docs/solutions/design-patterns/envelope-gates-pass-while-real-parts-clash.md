## Context

On 2026-10-07 three gates reported PASS or CLEAR for the cargo-bay side
walls: `cargo_layout_fit.py`, `landing_gear_wing_clearance.py --proud` and
`wing_root_deconflict.py`. `tools/cargo_bulkhead_context.py` then assembled
the real hull-frame meshes of both walls (58 solids, 1,653 pairs), including
the parts that are not printed, the harnesses and the door sweep. It found
53 clashes. The findings are recorded in `docs/CARGO_BULKHEAD_FIT_CHECK.md`.
Four were build-blocking:

- The bonded root flange is generated by its own script and was never bored,
  so the spar, the drive shaft and both harness ports run into solid plate.
- The fore gear-bay block sits on the CF spar and inside the wing.
- The starboard brake guide is a translated copy of the port part. The part
  is asymmetric about the worm axis, so the copy lands in the controller
  board.
- The latch pivot bracket sits inside the closed door.

Each gate compared its own idealisation against a shell, never one real part
against another. `cargo_layout_fit.py` subtracts its own bosses from the
shell and mirrors the parts that the assembly translates. The gear check
tests merged nominal features and accepted 12 mm proud with only a warning.

## Guidance

1. **Check the parts as generated, against each other.** A part that has its
   own generator (flange, collar, bay, bracket) must be in the pair census,
   not just reserved as a slab in the shell merge.
2. **Treat datums as claims to measure.** The payload keep-out sat at Z 8.72,
   but the measured closed-door floor is Z 10.86. When the box moved up, it
   met the aft nodes.
3. **Never translate a starboard part unless it is symmetric about the axis
   being copied.** Mirror it and print the mirror, or prove the symmetry.
   Test both with the census (here the translated guide overlapped 385 mm³;
   the mirrored one overlapped 0).
4. **Answer "is there a route?" with geometry.** Voxelise the free cavity
   (1 mm), take `scipy.ndimage.distance_transform_edt`, and label the
   components at radius ≥ bundle/2 + clearance. A breadth-first search then
   gives the path to export as a tube for the render. Here it found a 20 mm
   route through a shoulder pocket that no doc described.
5. **Point-in-solid from cross-sections needs even-odd parity.** A point
   inside a ring's hole is contained by two loops, and `count > 0` turns
   every collar into a plug. Hull sections also come back as separate wall
   strips, not nested loops. Decide "inside" by scanning outward from the
   centre plane until the first wall.
6. **Clip in Python, import in OpenSCAD.** OpenCSG preview of
   `intersection()` with these meshes draws false faces. Export clipped wall
   slabs and side-tagged `CLASH_<side>_nn.stl` files instead.

## Why This Matters

A green gate stops people from looking. Here a passing gate sat on top of a
spar that could not be inserted. Real parts, real datums and real copies are
where the defects were. None of them were visible to the idealisations.

## When to Apply

Before release of any part in a bay that more than one generator writes
into, and whenever a gate's model of the parts differs from the assembly's.

## Examples

```text
/usr/bin/python3 tools/cargo_bulkhead_context.py --corridor --export DIR
```
