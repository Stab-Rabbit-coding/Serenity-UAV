## 0. Design Summary

Each corner is a **canonical articulated leg** — cylinder-cluster thigh,
disc knee, slotted shin, disc ankle, tri-pad arrowhead foot — that **pivots
as one rigid printed frame about an M3 stainless hip pin** inside a
hull-flank bay. Two **spring** bowed wires (elastic, recoverable) and two
**ductile** bowed wires (plastic, sacrificial fuse) span the hip joint at a
6 mm bellcrank radius: leg flexion (foot swinging up-outboard) compresses
each wire along its chord so its pre-formed bow deepens — mechanically
identical to Rev R5. What the articulation adds is a **65 : 6 lever**
between foot and wires, so millimetres of wire stroke become tens of
millimetres of hull settle:

| Quantity | Rev R5 (rigid post) | **Rev R6 (hip-pivot)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Peak deceleration, worst case | ≈1,000 g | **52 g** (6 ft tail-down; 104 g level 4-point) |
| Hull settle absorbing the drop | ≈1.8 mm | **35 mm** (of 80 mm clearance) |
| Ground clearance | 31 mm (1.22 in) — failed payload req. | **80 mm (3.15 in)** — meets ≥76 mm (3.0 in) |
| Leg appearance | Thingiverse blade derivative | Canonical articulated leg, canonical bay stations |

**REDESIGN FOR CANONICAL HIGHT + flight safety only.**  The cargo loading doesn't require 3" ground cleanance.  (Aircraft lands over cargo with doors open.)

An M3 **extension-stop cross-pin** through the bay clevis ears carries the
opposite rotation (leg hanging under gravity, in-flight loads); landing
loads never touch it — they always run foot → hip pin + wires.

`airframe/stls/fuselage/landing-gear/lg_r6_*.stl`, §16).

## 1. Purpose and Requirements

The landing gear system must:

1. **Survive a vertical drop test onto hard level ground at design AUW**
   without cracking the hull shell. Height: **6 ft (1.829 m) baseline; a
   4 ft (1.219 m) reduction is under consideration** — §4.4 carries both
   schedules; the difference is wire stock diameter only. **Decision open:
   LG-17.**
2. **Absorb lateral loads** from not-quite-vertical landings (±15° design case).
3. **Fail internally, progressively, not catastrophically** — overload must
   produce defined, observable, partial degradation that still leaves the
   aircraft supported.
4. **Be field-replaceable** with common hand tools.
5. **Provide 3-D lateral stability** (tipping resistance both axes).
6. *(new in R6)* **Match the canonical Serenity landing gear** in form and
   in bay placement ([REF-CAD-003] Sheet 5; [REF-CAD-002]), deviating only
   where function demands (§2.4).
7. **[REJECTED]** 3x3x4 Cargo is carried internally.  Aircraft can lower over it with cargo bay doors fully open and out of the way. Extra hight beyond canon should be limited to what's required for flight safety.  Removal from Rev R1 was intentional.
   design (80 mm).

The landing gear does NOT need to be reusable after a worst-case drop
(ductile wires are consumables).

### 2.3 Load Share — the CG Correction

Build CG is at hull-frame **Y = +111.5 mm** (nacelle pivot = canonical
"Engine Pivots 360°" balance centre). Against the canonical wheelbase this
is **86% aft**:

```text
Static share: aft leg 43.1% each, fore leg 6.9% each
```

Canon agrees — the real ship is aft-heavy on its gear and additionally
deploys a "Cargo Bay Landing Pad" (Sheet 5 callout); on this build the
middle-section rear skids play that role (tip-back backstop; §10).
Tip-back margin about the aft-feet line: ≈12° to skid contact.
**Wire sizing is driven by the aft-leg worst case, one common schedule for
all corners** (fore legs simply carry large margins).

### 2.4 Canonical Form — Kept and Deviated

Kept (per [REF-CAD-003] Sheet 5 detail + [REF-CAD-002]): telescoping
cylinder-cluster thigh at ~53°, disc knee and ankle joints with the
4-satellite-hole wheel pattern, slotted flat shin, tri-pad arrowhead foot
with ribbed treads, recessed flank bays, "Feet Pivot 90°" (realized as a
square ankle spigot indexing the foot at 90° steps).

Deviations (functional, deliberate):

1. **Leg length** — canonical proportion gives ≈11 mm belly clearance at
   24-in scale; requirement 7 forces ≈2.5× longer legs (80 mm clearance).
2. **Articulation lives at the hip only** — knee/ankle discs are styling on
   a one-piece printed leg frame (print lying on its side: swing-plane
   bending stays in the layer plane).
3. **Fixed-down gear** — no retraction; the bay plate supplies the
   canonical recess look.

## 4. Mechanism and Wire Sizing

### 4.1 Bowed-Wire Model (carried from Rev R5, unchanged)

A straight wire with one shallow mid-span bow (rise h₀ = 3.5 mm), loaded
axially along its chord, collapses by deepening the bow via a hinge at the
crown (2-hinge model):

```text
P = 2M/h₀       M_plastic = σ_f d³/6      (ductile, flow stress σ_f = 550 MPa)
                M_elastic = σ_w π d³/32   (spring, working stress σ_w = 900 MPa)
stroke s = (h_f² − h₀²)/(2B)              (B = bow span; validity cap h_f ≤ 0.35 B)
U = P·s                                    (constant-force plateau approximation)
```

**New in R6:** wire ends stay **straight** (socket seat depth + 2 mm run-in
per end) so they seat in straight bores; only the exposed span B carries
the bow. Stock length L = B + 2·(seat + 2 mm).

### 4.2 The Hip Lever

Wires act at bellcrank radius **r = 6 mm** about the hip pin; the foot's
ground force acts at horizontal arm **R_h = 65 mm**. Ratio **10.8 : 1**:

```text
Plateau ground force per leg   F = 2 P r / R_h        (ductile pair engaged)
Hull settle                    d = U_leg / F
Wire stroke                    s = d · r / R_h
```

The wires see Rev R5-class forces (kN) and millimetre strokes; the hull
sees hundreds of newtons over tens of millimetres. Peak deceleration is a
free design choice via r — set here to keep the worst case near 50 g.

### 4.3 Spring Pair (elastic phase — identical for both drop options)

```text
Sized to absorb the ordinary-hard-landing energy fully elastically:
  per-leg elastic capacity 3.51 J (per wire 1.755 J)
  d = 3.35 mm, bow span 23 mm, stock L = 37 mm, h₀ = 3.5 mm
  P = 1,896 N at elastic limit → 350 N/leg; stroke 0.93 mm (avail 1.14, 1.2× reserve)
  elastic settle at limit 10.0 mm; onset deceleration 46 g (level)
  mass 2.56 g × 8 = 20.5 g/aircraft
```

### 4.4 Ductile Pair — the 6 ft vs 4 ft Decision (LG-17)

Both schedules share **bow span 55 mm, stock L = 75 mm, h₀ = 3.5 mm, and
every printed part unchanged** — the choice is wire stock diameter only:

| | **6 ft schedule (baseline)** | **4 ft schedule** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Worst-leg energy (tail-down) | 28.1 J | 18.7 J |
| Ductile wire diameter | **Ø4.36 mm** | **Ø3.81 mm** |
| Wire collapse force P | 4,333 N | 2,889 N |
| Plateau ground force / leg | 800 N (180 lbf) | 533 N (120 lbf) |
| Hull settle (worst leg) | 35.1 mm | 35.1 mm |
| Hip rotation at full stroke | 30.9° | 30.9° |
| Peak decel — tail-down / level | **52 g / 104 g** | **35 g / 70 g** |
| Wire mass ×8 | 70.2 g | 53.6 g |
| 6 ft drop capability | 1.0× (design point) | **not covered** (0.67×) |
| 4 ft drop capability | 1.5× margin | 1.0× (design point) |

Decision guidance: the 6 ft schedule flown and **tested at 4 ft**
demonstrates a 1.5× energy margin with a 23 mm settle — arguably the best
of both. Choosing the 4 ft schedule trades away 6 ft survivability for
~17 g lower worst-case deceleration and 17 g less wire mass. Either way
the airframe geometry is frozen — **LG-15 procurement is the only gate.**

### 4.5 Stroke Reserve and Fired Shape

Full stroke uses 3.24 mm of the 3.26 mm model-validity reserve; the fired
ductile bow rises to ≈19 mm over the 55 mm span — a **visibly bent,
unambiguous field-inspection indicator** (`lg_r6_ductile_wire_deformed.stl`).
During full flex the foot skids ≈45 mm outboard; TPU treads slide rather
than grab — intended.

### 4.6 Structure Checks (at 6 ft-schedule plateau, the harsher case)

```text
Leg frame @ hip:  M = 52.0 N·m; section 2×Ø14 mm cylinders @ 18 mm ctrs + web
                  Z = 2,220 mm³ → σ = 23.4 MPa vs 27.5 MPa allowable
                  (allowable = CF-PETG yield/2) → 1.17× on top of 2× = 2.3× total
Hip pin:          M3 stainless, double shear 4,241 N vs 800 N → 5.3×
Bay boss bearing: Ø11 boss / wire bore, 70 MPa → 6,300 N vs P = 4,333 N → 1.45×
Lateral ±15°:     214 N/leg into hip pin + clevis side faces (pin margin above)
Static stance:    35.8 N/wire (aft corner) vs 4,333 N plateau — stance is rigid
```

Leg frame print: CF-PETG, **100% infill**, 0.15 mm layers, **lying on the
−Y face** so swing-plane bending stays in the layer plane.

## 6. Progressive Failure / Fuse Strategy (Rev R6)

| Level | Element | Activates at | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (elastic, no damage) | Spring pair + leg frame | ≤350 N/leg (spring elastic limit) | Fully recoverable; ordinary hard landings, zero permanent set; ≤10 mm springy settle |
| 2 (primary sacrificial fuse) | Ductile pair | 800 N/leg plateau (6 ft schedule) | Bows deepen progressively over 35 mm settle; visibly bent afterward; field-replaceable stock |
| 3 (rotation limit) | Belly + 80 mm clearance | beyond full stroke | 45 mm residual clearance at full flex — hull still clear of ground |
| 4 (lateral retention) | Wire socket retention (LG-13) + hip pin | §4.6 lateral case | Wires cannot walk out of sockets; pin 5.3× margin |
| 5 (protected) | Bay bosses / cargo shell | >1.45× wire collapse | Hull is not expected to see damaging load |

The leg frame is not expected to yield at any credible load (2.3× total
margin at plateau); **either ductile wire alone still arrests the aircraft**
(at double settle, 70 mm — consuming the clearance reserve, level 3).

## 13. Assembly Procedure

1. **Bays:** bolt 4 bay plates to the cargo-shell flanks at the §2.2
   stations (4× M3 + internal backing plates each; LG-02 conforming detail).
2. **Wires:** form 16 + spares per §11.2/11.3 on the bending jig (straight
   ends, mid-span bow); temper check per LG-15/LG-16.
3. **Leg:** seat foot on spigot (choose 90° index), install M2.5 cross-bolt.
4. **Hang:** set leg lug between clevis ears, drive hip pin; install
   extension-stop cross-pin; leg should hang freely against the stop.
5. **Wires in:** seat each wire leg-end in its bellcrank socket and bay-end
   in its boss (spring outers first), apply LG-13 retention.
6. **Safety cord:** foot hub → bay → hull anchor.
7. Repeat ×4; verify all four feet touch on a flat plate (Z-level rule:
   shim to the lowest foot).

## 14. Field Replacement (after a hard landing)

1. Inspect: fired ductile wires show a deep (~19 mm) bow — replace both on
   any fired corner. Spring wires must show zero set — replace if bent.
2. Release LG-13 retention, withdraw fired wires, seat replacements
   (cut-to-length stock + bend jig, or pre-formed spares).
3. Inspect leg frame, pins, bay bosses (no yield expected, §4.6);
   check foot spigot bolt.

**Tools:** 2 mm and 2.5 mm hex keys, pin punch, wire bend jig, side cutters.
